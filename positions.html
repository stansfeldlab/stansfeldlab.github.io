Warwick scholarships

These are all applied for through Warwick’s PGR Scholarship Competitions, which give full details, benefits and eligibility for each scheme.

  • Faculty Chancellor’s Scholarships – fees and stipend for 3.5 years, open to any nationality
  • Interdisciplinary Chancellor’s Scholarships – for interdisciplinary projects
  • Doctoral Access – Pathway – for applicants who identify as Black or of Black heritage
  • Doctoral Access – Sanctuary – for applicants with a UK asylum-related status
  • China Scholarship Council – for People’s Republic of China passport holders
