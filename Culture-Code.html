    How to play

    Five samples from a hospital outbreak have grown bacteria. Work out which ESKAPE pathogen is in each sample, in ten rounds or fewer.

    Tap a bug to put it in the next dish, then tap Check. Each round gives you one peg per bug:

    • Right bug in the right sample
    • Right bug in the wrong sample
    • No match

    The pegs are not in dish order, so you have to work out which bugs they refer to.

    Each outbreak also gives you three lab tests. Tap a covered sample to test it. The lid then shows which bugs still fit.

    Bold marks a result that only one of the six gives. Hospital labs now often identify bacteria by mass spectrometry (MALDI-TOF), but these quick tests are still the classic first steps.

    ESKAPE stands for Enterococcus faecium, Staphylococcus aureus, Klebsiella pneumoniae, Acinetobacter baumannii, Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Enterobacter species.
