Industry partners

  • What we do together: Designing Bicycle binders to novel cell envelope targets, as part of the search for new antimicrobials. The collaboration supports an MRC DTP PhD student and postdoctoral research in the group.
  • Related grant: Antimicrobial Resistance: Breakthrough Compound Discovery through Mechanistic Studies combined with Bicycle Technology and Target Validation (BBSRC, UKRI7041), November 2023 to April 2027

Computing

Our simulations rely on national and regional high-performance computing. We gratefully acknowledge:

  • Warwick’s Scientific Computing Research Technology Platform (SCRTP), for local computing including the Avon cluster.
  • Sulis, the Tier 2 HPC platform hosted by SCRTP at the University of Warwick, funded by EPSRC (EP/T022108/1) and the HPC Midlands+ consortium.
  • HECBioSim, the UK High-End Computing Consortium for Biomolecular Simulation, which provides access to national supercomputers such as ARCHER2, with support from EPSRC.
