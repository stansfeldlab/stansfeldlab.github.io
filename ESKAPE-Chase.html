The ESKAPE panel

    Ward rules

    Combination therapy
    The four two-tone doses pair a β-lactam with a partner drug. Every bacterium that isn't fully resistant turns vulnerable.
    Finish the course
    A bacterium that survives a dose gains a level of resistance. At three it ignores combination therapy. Lysed bacteria regrow susceptible from the reservoir.
    Plasmid transfer
    When two Gram-negatives, or two Gram-positives, touch, both take the higher resistance level.
    Biofilm
    P. aeruginosa leaves green biofilm that slows your capsule.
    Bacteriophage
    Appears twice per plate below the reservoir. Lyses the nearest bacterium, however resistant.
