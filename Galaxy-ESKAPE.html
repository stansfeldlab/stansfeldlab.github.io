      How it builds

      • Waves 1–3: meet the six ESKAPE pathogens and E. coli. Amoxicillin kills them all for now.
      • Waves 4–10: one pathogen per wave starts resisting. Its coloured dot shows the drug, which unlocks if you need it.
      • Waves 11–13: MRSA, ESBL Klebsiella and CRAB bosses. Two hits each.
      • Combination therapy: a few bugs each wave carry a capsule. Shoot one and catch the capsule for 10 seconds of twin syringes. Your last two drugs fire together, wrong-drug darts pass by harmlessly, and the second syringe takes one hit for you.
      • Wave 14 on: resisted hits divide and the copy dives at you.
      • Scoring: narrowest drug +100, double on divers. Broad drug +40 and pressure up; colistin counts double.
      • Hits: each toxin or ramming bug adds 0.5 °C. 40.0 °C ends the game.
