Membrane Protein Complexes in E. coli

List of all membrane protein complexes in Escherichia coli, strain K12, that are listed on Complex Portal. For complexes where the stoichiometry of one or more protein was unknown, various different cases were modelled and what was considered most plausible is displayed here. In these cases, the stoichiometry is listed on the card. Note that some predictions do not align with the stoichiometry given on Complex Portal.

Click the accession code (CPX-0000) to view the Complex Portal entry. Clicking the image of the complex opens the Mol* interactive viewer in a new tab. Within this, the structure with and without the signal peptide can be viewed (if applicable), as well as the structure placed within a membrane; these can be toggled in the ‘State Tree’ on the left-hand-side menu.

AlphaFold confidence scores, ipTM and pTM, are displayed; AlphaFold pLDDT colouring can be seen in Mol*. The Download button fetches the complex together with its MemPrO and CG2AT membranes.
