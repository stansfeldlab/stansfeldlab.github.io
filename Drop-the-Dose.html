    How to play

    1. Read the case and load the drug you think will work.
    2. Swipe across a tube to cut it, or press 1 2 3 to cut tubes in order. R restarts.
    3. Land the capsule on the cells. Pick up all three doses on the way for a full course.

    A teaching game, not treatment advice. Real choices depend on the isolate’s susceptibility results and local guidelines.
