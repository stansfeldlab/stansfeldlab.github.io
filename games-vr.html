Games & VR Demos

Browser games about membrane proteins and antimicrobial resistance, plus 3D structures to explore in VR.

Games

Don’t Get SolubilisedCatch solutes and ions and carry them through to the cytoplasm, grab helpful lipids, and shake off the baddies before you’re solubilised.

Don’t Get Solubilised XRMixed-reality version for Meta Quest 3: feed Mr Membrane Protein solutes and ions and swat the detergents. Mouse mode too.

Active Site SorterRoll each piece over the protein to find the one ligand that binds its active site. VR supported.

Drop the DoseSix ESKAPE pathogens: read the case, load the drug that gets through, then cut the tubes to land it on the cells.

ESKAPE ChaseA maze chase: one β-lactam capsule against the six hospital pathogens best at escaping antibiotics.

ESKAPE InvadersMatch the drug to the Gram stain as resistance spreads wave by wave.

Galaxy ESKAPERight drug, right bug: a space shooter where each new pathogen needs the right antibiotic.

SuperBugsPull back the plunger, aim and fire antibiotics to eradicate the ESKAPE pathogens.

Superbug ShowdownMatch-three against superbugs: rotate drugs to keep selection pressure low and avert the antibiotic apocalypse.

Superbug SmackdownWhack each ESKAPE pathogen with a drug its shields don’t show, before the infection takes hold.

ESKAPE ContainmentWin plates to earn stars, then spend them on fixes to contain ESKAPE cultures growing where they shouldn’t.

Culture CodeCrack the outbreak code: use peg clues and real lab tests to work out which ESKAPE pathogen grew in each of five samples.
