How to play

  • Drag back from anywhere on the slide and let go to fire. The dotted guide shows the path before you commit.
  • Keyboard: arrows move and aim, hold Space to charge, Enter jumps, 1–7 picks a drug.
  • Each drug button shows S, I or R against every enemy colony. Resistant cells take a fraction of the damage.
  • Blasts still push resistant cells around. Knock them into the bleach at the bottom of the slide.
  • Missiles and the phage cocktail drift with the flow. Grenades bounce and pop after 3 seconds.
  • For a neutrophil strike, tap where it should land.
